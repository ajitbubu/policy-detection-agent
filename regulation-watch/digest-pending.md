## 1. EU–US Data Privacy Framework — adequacy under renewed legal pressure

- **Jurisdiction:** EU / US (cross-border transfer mechanism, Global cross-border scope)
- **Why it matters:** US Supreme Court's *Trump v. Slaughter* ruling (decided 2026-06-29) removed
  for-cause removal protection for independent agency heads, reviving concerns about the
  independence of the Privacy and Civil Liberties Oversight Board (PCLOB), which has lacked
  quorum since three members were removed in January 2025. Privacy advocacy group NOYB has
  asked the European Commission to withdraw the DPF adequacy decision and has announced plans
  to litigate before the CJEU. The adequacy decision remains formally in force today — no
  binding change yet — but this is the clearest near-term threat to the legal basis many
  DataSafeguard customers rely on for US-bound tracking/analytics data. **Escalate to a full
  alert immediately if:** the European Commission suspends/reviews the adequacy decision, or
  NOYB's CJEU case is formally lodged/decided.
- **Primary source:** https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/reports-statements-and-letters/edpb-report-on-the-first-review-of-the-european-commission_en
- **Secondary refs:** https://noyb.eu/en/us-supreme-court-just-blew-eu-us-data-transfers , https://www.activemind.legal/guides/dpf-supreme-court/ , https://btlj.org/2026/02/third-times-the-charm-the-fate-of-the-eu-u-s-data-privacy-framework/
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM — Supreme Court ruling and PCLOB quorum facts are well-corroborated; consequence for DPF adequacy is speculative/pending litigation, not yet a binding change.

## 2. Australia — Privacy Act Tranche 2 exposure draft (targeted advertising, tracking tech)

- **Jurisdiction:** Australia
- **Why it matters:** On 2026-08-31 the Australian Government released the Consultation Paper
  and Exposure Draft for the Privacy Amendment (Personal Data Protection) Bill 2026 — the
  second reform tranche, with ~40 proposals including a new "fair and reasonable" collection/use
  standard and explicit obligations for targeted digital advertising, wearables, and connected
  vehicles. Submissions closed 2026-09-18. This is still an exposure draft (not introduced or
  passed), so no effective date exists yet, but it directly foreshadows Vendor/SDK Governance
  and Consent Signals work for the AU ruleset. **Escalate when:** the Bill is formally introduced
  to Parliament with a start/transition date.
- **Primary source:** (Attorney-General's Department consultation page — official .gov.au domain;

## 3. Colombia — Statutory Bill No. 282 of 2026 (GDPR-style rewrite, explicit cookie/tracking consent clause)

- **Jurisdiction:** Colombia
- **Why it matters:** Bill 282/2026, published in the Congressional Gazette on 2026-09-08, would
  rewrite Colombia's 2012 privacy law (Law 1581) with an EU-style extraterritorial scope clause,
  fines up to 5% of annual operating revenue, and an explicit provision prohibiting collection/use
  of data via cookies or similar online tracking tools without the data subject's consent. Still
  at bill stage (not enacted); worth tracking closely given the direct, unambiguous cookie-consent
  language. **Escalate when:** the bill passes a legislative chamber or is enacted/gazetted as law.
- **Primary source:** Gaceta del Congreso de Colombia (official gazette; not independently
  fetch-verified this run)
- **Secondary refs:** https://ppc.land/colombias-third-data-bill-would-fine-firms-up-to-5-of-revenue/ , https://www.consentstack.io/regulations/co-law1581
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM — bill's existence and headline terms corroborated by an independent
  tracker; full bill text not independently retrieved this run.

## 4. Argentina — competing bills to overhaul Law 25.326 (GDPR/LGPD alignment)

- **Jurisdiction:** Argentina
- **Why it matters:** Three bills are simultaneously pending in Congress to replace/overhaul
  Law 25.326 (Carro 1948-D-2025, Doñate 644-S-2025, and Rossi 3397-D-2026, the last filed
  2026-07-16), all drawing on an AAIP-drafted proposal and aiming to align Argentina with
  GDPR/LGPD-style rules. Early-stage and fragmented (three competing texts, no clear front-runner)
  — awareness only for now, but relevant to LatAm jurisdiction-routing roadmap. **Escalate when:**
  one bill is consolidated/reported out of committee with a floor vote scheduled.
- **Primary source:** https://www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip/datospersonales/proyecto-ley-datos-personales
- **Secondary refs:** https://iapp.org/news/a/novedades-legislativas-en-argentina-sobre-protecci-n-de-datos-personales-e-inteligencia-artificial , https://allende.com/privacidad-y-ciberseguridad/se-reintroduce-en-el-congreso-el-proyecto-de-reforma-integral-del-regimen-argentino-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales-08-12-2026/
