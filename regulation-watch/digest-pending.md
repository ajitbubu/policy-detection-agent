### Japan — APPI amendment promulgated
- Instrument: Amendment to the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI)
- Summary: Passed the Diet 2026-07-10, promulgated 2026-07-17. Introduces a new consent exemption for statistical/AI-training processing of public sensitive data, a broader "no prejudice to rights/interests" consent exception, and new child-specific protections (parental consent required under age 16, enhanced deletion/suspension rights for minors). Comes into force within 2 years of promulgation (by 2028 at the latest); exact effective date not yet set.
- Source: https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/05/japan-appi-reform-key-changes (SECONDARY — pending primary confirmation; primary is Japan's e-Gov law portal, not yet checked)
- Why LOW: Effective date likely >180 days out (possibly ~2 years); relevant to Consent Banner (child-directed consent age gating) and Cookie Classification ML (new consent-exemption categories) once implementing timeline firms up. Re-scan when Cabinet Office/PPC sets the commencement date.
