# LOW-Severity Digest — Pending Monday Weekly Digest

Items below are awareness-only (LOW severity per rubric: effective >180 days, not yet enacted, or clarifies existing behavior). Appended by the Tier 2 weekly scan. Cleared and emailed by the Monday weekly Routine.

- **[APAC] Japan — APPI amendment bill submitted to Diet, April 2026.** Not yet enacted. Proposed changes would remove the opt-in-consent requirement for AI-training data sharing (new consent exemption for statistical/AI processing) and remove the existing 5,000-individual small-business threshold, plus a risk-based individual-breach-notification exemption. Watch for passage — would be HIGH if enacted given the AI-training consent exemption's interplay with any AI-driven personalization features in vendor/SDK data flows. Source: https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/05/japan-appi-reform-key-changes (SECONDARY — pending primary confirmation via ppc.go.jp).

- **[APAC] Australia — Privacy Act reform "Tranche 2" still uncommitted.** Attorney-General confirmed (Feb 2026 Senate estimates) the government is "progressing" a Tranche 2 Bill, but no bill has been introduced and no commencement date is set. Proposed content (fair-and-reasonable processing test, GDPR-style right to erasure, removal of small-business/employee exemptions, expanded "personal information" definition to cover online identifiers) would be HIGH-impact for Cookie Classification ML and Consent Banner if/when introduced. Source: https://rulesmate.com.au/insights/privacy-act-second-tranche-reforms-2026-outlook (SECONDARY — no bill yet, nothing to confirm against primary).

- **[APAC] Thailand — PDPA amendment not yet enacted; PDPC opened public consultation March 2026** on forthcoming guidelines (legal bases, security/breach notification, DPO obligations, direct marketing, RoPA, CCTV/national-ID collection). No cookie-specific change identified yet. Source: PDPC consultation announcement, referenced via https://www.recordinglaw.com/world-laws/world-data-privacy-laws/thailand-data-privacy-laws/ (SECONDARY — pending primary confirmation via pdpc.or.th).

- **[MENA/Africa] Kenya — Data Protection (Amendment) Bill, 2025 still pending.** Would expand sensitive-data categories (political opinions, trade union membership), create a Data Protection Appeals Tribunal, and raise the fine cap from "lower of KES 5M or 1%" to "higher of." Not yet enacted. Source: https://wamaeallen.com/summary-of-the-data-protection-amendment-bill-2025/ (SECONDARY).

- **[MENA/Africa] Nigeria — NDPC Compliance Audit Return (CAR) filing deadline for 2025 activities** (31 March 2026, reportedly extended to 30 May 2026 per one source) has already passed; this is a data-controller filing obligation, not a CMP/cookie-consent delta. No action needed from ID-PRIVACY® directly; noted for awareness that NDPC enforcement posture is expected to shift from education to enforcement through 2026. Source: https://www.templars-law.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Client-Alert-Data-Protection-Compliance-in-Nigeria.pdf (SECONDARY).

- **[LatAm] Mexico — LFPDPPP 2025 reform implementing regulations still pending.** The Secretariat of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance (replacing dissolved INAI) began stakeholder dialogues in January 2026; no revised regulations or technical corrections have been published in the Official Gazette as of this scan. Watch for publication — would likely require Cross-border Transfer Mechanism and Documentation subsystem updates once issued. Source: https://iapp.org/news/a/new-authority-established-for-personal-data-protection-in-mexico (SECONDARY).

- **[LatAm] Argentina — Multiple Personal Data Protection Bill drafts remain in Congress**, proposing mandatory breach notification, fines up to 4% of global turnover, and an accountability principle. Timing and final shape uncertain; AAIP continues to issue non-binding AI-transparency instruments in the interim. Not yet enacted. Source: https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=f5ce9cc0-2598-453b-920e-11954ae9b32d (SECONDARY).
