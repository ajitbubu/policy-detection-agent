### LOW-1 — Colombia: new data protection bill filed with Congress
- **Jurisdiction:** Colombia
- **Instrument:** Government bill to modernize Statutory Law 1581 of 2012 (data protection), filed with the Cámara de Representantes
- **Published:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** Bill introduction only — no committee schedule, no effective date. Sponsored jointly by the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC), MinCiencias, and the Procuraduría General.
- **Why it matters for cookies/consent:** Would modernize the 13-year-old law with updated definitions (biometric data, genetic data, profiling, automated decisions) that likely touch online identifiers/tracking; no cookie-specific provisions surfaced yet in public materials.
- **Action:** Awareness only. Re-check in ~60 days for committee movement.

### LOW-2 — Japan: APPI amendment enacted (Law No. 56), but main-body effective date is ~2028
- **Jurisdiction:** Japan
- **Instrument:** Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) amendment, Law No. 56 of 2026 — new Article 31-2 "contactable personally referable information" (explicitly includes cookie IDs, phone numbers, email addresses)
- **Published:** 2026-07-17 (promulgated, after passing House of Representatives 2026-05-26 and House of Councillors 2026-07-10)
- **Status:** Enacted, but the main body's commencement date is to be fixed by cabinet order within 2 years of promulgation; the Personal Information Protection Commission's (PPC) implementation roadmap currently pencils in spring–July 2028. Effective date is >180 days out, so this is LOW per severity rubric despite being directly relevant to cookie-identifier governance.
- **Why it matters for cookies/consent:** Extends APPI-style prohibitions (improper use, unauthorized acquisition) to cookie IDs and other "contactable" identifiers even where they don't meet the full "personal information" definition — this is a genuine future Vendor/SDK Governance and Consent Signals scope item for Japan.
- **Action:** Add to the next monthly Tier 3 / horizon report as a 2027–2028 watch item. No engineering action now.
- **Source (secondary):** https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/05/japan-appi-reform-key-changes ; https://www.fisherphillips.com/en/insights/insights/japanese-cabinet-approves-appi-amendments ; https://iapp.org/news/a/navigating-japan-s-proposed-appi-amendments-key-timelines-open-issues-and-action-points

- **Instrument:** Establishment of the UAE Artificial Intelligence and Data Authority, consolidating the former UAE Data Office (which enforced the PDPL, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) with AI/digital-economy functions

- **Status:** Institutional restructuring, not a substantive rule change. No new cookie/consent obligation identified. Older news (June) that does not appear in the confirmed-duplicate list from prior runs — flagged now for completeness rather than urgency.
