WHAT CHANGED
---------------------------------------------------------------
IAB Europe published a public-comment draft of TCF technical specification changes to
support TCF Policy Version 5.0.b on 29 May 2026 (comment window closes 29 June 2026).
The primary change requires CMPs to display standardised feature explanation text --
alongside the feature's standard name and full user-friendly description -- in the CMP
UI for every TCF feature. This text will be included in the Global Vendor List (GVL) as
a new `standardTexts` object with translations. The change responds to persistent user
research finding that users do not understand the distinction between TCF "purposes" (which
carry opt-in/opt-out controls) and TCF "features" (which currently carry no user controls,
only disclosure). CMPs that are already compliant with TCF 2.3 must layer in this additional
UI disclosure requirement by mid-October 2026 for web and mid-February 2027 for native app
environments.

RISK IF NOT ACTIONED
---------------------------------------------------------------
CMPs that do not implement the required feature disclosures by mid-October 2026 risk
losing IAB Europe TCF CMP certification, which would prevent certified use of TCF strings
for programmatic advertising by customers. Loss of certification would be a significant
commercial risk for any customer relying on TCF for consent-based programmatic revenue.
Fine risk is indirect (via DPA scrutiny of non-compliant consent strings) but the CJEU
and Brussels Court of Appeal rulings in 2025 establishing IAB Europe as joint controller
mean regulator attention on TCF compliance is elevated.
