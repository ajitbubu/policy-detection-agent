---------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT CHANGED
---------------------------------------------------------------
The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (Royal Assent June 2025, key PECR provisions in force
5 February 2026) restructured PECR in two material ways. First, PECR now covers ALL storage
and access technologies by function -- not just cookies -- including tracking pixels, device
fingerprinting, web storage, and tag-based scripts. Second, the Act introduced three new
consent-exempt categories beyond the pre-existing "communication" and "strictly necessary"
exemptions: (a) Statistical -- collecting aggregate visitor statistics solely to improve the
service; (b) Appearance -- adapting the UI to a user's stored preferences; and (c) Emergency
Assistance -- locating a user at their request for emergency services. The ICO published its
final, authoritative guidance on 29 April 2026 operationalising all five exceptions. Critically,
for Statistical and Appearance exceptions the site must still provide users a "simple and free
means of objecting," and the exception is purpose-limited (analytics feeding ad targeting do
not qualify). The maximum PECR fine rose from GBP 500,000 to GBP 17.5 million or 4% of global
annual turnover (whichever is higher), aligning penalties with UK GDPR.

---------------------------------------------------------------
WHO IS AFFECTED
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Applicability test:  Any organisation that places or accesses information on the terminal
  equipment of users located in the UK, regardless of establishment. Targeting test applies.
- DataSafeguard customers likely affected: All customers operating or targeting UK users (broad
  segment -- likely a large portion of the customer base for any publisher, retailer, or
  adtech platform). UK tenant configurations are materially affected.
- Customer notice required: YES -- by 2026-07-04 (T-30 advisory). Customers must update their
  cookie audits, reclassify technologies previously outside PECR scope (pixels, fingerprinting),
  and either re-categorise exempt cookies or retain/obtain consent. Customers relying on the
  new Statistical or Appearance exemptions must add a "simple means of objecting" UI element.

---------------------------------------------------------------
ID-PRIVACY IMPACT
---------------------------------------------------------------
Affected subsystems:
  [X] Consent Banner               -- (1) Appearance and Statistical exempt cookies may now
                                       default to "on" in UK banners, but a simple opt-out
                                       toggle/link must be present on the banner or a clearly
                                       linked settings page. Banner layer logic for
                                       jurisdiction == UK must be updated. (2) PECR scope
                                       expansion means the banner must surface categories for
                                       pixels, fingerprinting, and tag-based scripts, not only
                                       cookie categories.
  [X] Cookie Classification ML     -- Classifier must recognise five PECR exception categories
                                       as distinct classification outputs for UK jurisdiction:
                                       Communication, Strictly Necessary, Statistical, Appearance,
                                       Emergency. Training data needs UK-specific labelling.
                                       Non-cookie trackers (pixels, fingerprinting scripts) must
                                       now be auto-classified into this framework.
  [X] Consent Storage & Proof      -- For cookies relying on new Statistical/Appearance
                                       exemptions, consent is not captured -- but the "simple
                                       means of objecting" signal must be logged to demonstrate
                                       no objection was received. Proof schema needs a new
                                       field: `pecdExemption` + `objectionSignalStatus`.
  [ ] Consent Signals (GPC/TCF/GPP)-- No direct change; existing GPC handling covers opt-out
                                       for non-exempt categories.
  [X] Geo / Jurisdiction Routing   -- UK routing ruleset must be updated to (a) apply the
                                       DUAA five-exception model instead of the pre-DUAA two-
                                       exception model, and (b) expand technology scope from
                                       "cookies" to "storage and access technologies".
  [X] Vendor / SDK Governance      -- Tracking pixels and fingerprinting SDKs previously outside
                                       PECR scope are now in scope. Vendor taxonomy must flag
                                       these. Pre-consent gating logic for UK must include them.
  [ ] DSR Engine                   -- No direct change.
  [X] Audit / Logging              -- Evidence of "simple means of objecting" being offered and
                                       not exercised (for exempt categories) must be logged.
                                       Audit report template for UK needs new exception-status
                                       column.
  [ ] Cross-border transfer        -- Not directly implicated by this change.
  [X] Tenant Config / Admin UI     -- New UK-specific toggle set: enable/disable Statistical
                                       exception, enable/disable Appearance exception (with
                                       mandatory objection mechanism if enabled). UI must warn
                                       that purpose-limited use is required.
  [X] Documentation / DPIA / RoPA  -- UK PECR scope statement in DPIAs and RoPA entries for
                                       UK processing must be updated to reference DUAA and the
                                       five-exception model. Template language update required.

RISK IF NOT ACTIONED
---------------------------------------------------------------
Maximum PECR fine is now GBP 17.5 million or 4% of global annual turnover -- a 35-fold
increase from the prior GBP 500,000 ceiling. The ICO has signalled active enforcement
posture on cookie compliance. Customers found to have expanded their non-consent cookie
use (e.g., analytics) without implementing the "simple means of objecting" risk direct ICO
investigation. DataSafeguard contractual liability to customers for CMP misconfiguration
is also implicated. The ICO's track record on PECR enforcement has strengthened since 2023.
