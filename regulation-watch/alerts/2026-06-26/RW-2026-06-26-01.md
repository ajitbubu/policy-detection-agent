WHAT CHANGED (plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
South Korea's National Assembly promulgated a major overhaul of PIPA on 10 March 2026, with most provisions effective 11 September 2026. The amendment lifts the maximum administrative penalty ceiling from 3% to 10% of total global turnover for aggravated violations (repeated willful misconduct, breaches affecting 10 million+ data subjects, or non-compliance with a PIPC corrective order). The CEO is now explicitly designated as the "ultimate responsible person" for personal data protection, and for entities meeting size thresholds set in Presidential Decree, the Chief Privacy Officer appointment must be Board-approved and formally reported to the PIPC. The amendment also expands the definition of "breach" to include forgery, alteration, and damage to personal information — not merely loss or leakage. For cookies/tracking specifically, the existing PIPA rule that explicit opt-in consent is required when cookie data is combinable with other data to identify an individual remains, and privacy policies must explicitly disclose all automatic data collection devices used.
