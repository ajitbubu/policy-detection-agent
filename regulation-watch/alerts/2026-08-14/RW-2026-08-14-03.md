───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
China's PIPL cross-border transfer regime previously offered two pathways: mandatory CAC Security Assessment (for CIIOs and large-volume exporters) and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for mid-volume exporters. The new Certification Measures, effective 2026-01-01, add a third pathway: third-party certification by an accredited body, available to non-CIIO exporters transferring 100,000-1,000,000 individuals' personal data (or under 10,000 individuals' sensitive data) per year, with certificates valid for 3 years. A second wave of supplementary national standards covering technical controls and certification-body accreditation became mandatory 2026-07-01, completing the operational detail needed to use this pathway. This is a new optional compliance route, not a replacement of SCCs/Security Assessment — existing DataSafeguard customers using those routes face no forced migration.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHO IS AFFECTED
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Applicability test:  Non-CIIO personal information handlers in China transferring 100K-1M individuals' data (or <10K sensitive) abroad annually who want a certification-based alternative to SCCs.
- DataSafeguard customers likely affected: Customers with China (CN) jurisdiction routing and mid-scale cross-border transfer volumes who are currently using SCCs and may prefer certification for audit/commercial reasons — estimate: a minority of CN-jurisdiction tenants, opt-in only.
- Customer notice required:  NO — this is a new optional mechanism, not a compliance deadline. Awareness-level product note is sufficient; no forced customer action.
