WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The DPDP Rules phase in over 18 months. On 2026-11-13 the Consent Manager registration regime becomes operative: entities offering a platform for data principals to give, review, manage and withdraw consent can register with the Data Protection Board. This creates a regulated interoperability layer that CMPs and data fiduciaries may need to integrate with. Core fiduciary notice and consent duties follow on 2027-05-13.
