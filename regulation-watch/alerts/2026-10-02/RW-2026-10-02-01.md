WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AB 566 amends the CCPA so that, from 2027-01-01, any business that develops or maintains a web browser must offer an easy-to-find setting that sends an opt-out preference signal (e.g. GPC) to every site visited. Businesses already had to honor such signals under CPPA regulations; this adds a statutory basis and shifts signal generation to browser vendors. Expect materially higher GPC signal volume (including from default-capable mainstream browsers) hitting customer sites. The CPPA has also voted (Aug 2026) to begin formal rulemaking naming GPC in the regulations; no notice of proposed rulemaking yet.
