---------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT CHANGED
---------------------------------------------------------------
On 29 May 2026, IAB Europe and IAB Tech Lab jointly announced TCF Policy v5.0.b
and the corresponding TCF v2.4 technical specification (GitHub PR #443), advancing
from the current v5.0.a / TCF v2.3. Three substantive changes are introduced:
(1) Multi-device consent handling -- new requirements for how consent signals are
carried when an authenticated user moves across devices, aligned with CNIL
multi-device guidance; (2) New CMP UI requirements -- Chapter V, Appendix B(B)(f)
now mandates that CMPs display a standard explanation text and illustration
alongside each Feature listed in the consent layer (Features are means of
processing, distinct from Purposes); (3) Renamed Special Feature 2 -- previously
"Use precise geolocation data and actively scan device characteristics for
identification," now renamed "Identify devices based on information actively
requested," reflecting updated CNIL and EDPB framing of active fingerprinting.
A public comment period runs until 29 June 2026.
