---------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT CHANGED
---------------------------------------------------------------
The New Zealand Privacy Amendment Act 2025 (Royal Assent 24 September 2025)
introduced a new Information Privacy Principle 3A (IPP 3A), which came into force
on 1 May 2026 and applies only to personal information collected on or after that
date. IPP 3A requires agencies to take reasonable steps to notify individuals when
their personal data is collected indirectly -- i.e., from a third party rather
than from the individual themselves. The notification must disclose: what was
collected, the purpose of collection, who holds it, and the individual's rights of
access and correction. This must occur as soon as reasonably practicable. The
principle is relevant to ID-PRIVACY in contexts where customer websites use third-
party data enrichment, audience segmentation, or data broker integrations that
feed into their consent or targeting stack -- these flows now require proactive
notification to the data subject in New Zealand.

---------------------------------------------------------------
WHO IS AFFECTED
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Applicability test:   Agencies (organisations) that collect personal
                        information of New Zealand individuals indirectly
                        (from a third party). Broad extraterritorial reach
                        under Privacy Act 2020 for offshore organisations
                        "carrying on business" in NZ.
- DataSafeguard customers likely affected: Customers who (a) operate in NZ
  or target NZ users AND (b) use third-party data feeds, SDK-level audience
  data, or data broker integrations. This is a subset of the customer base.
- Customer notice required: YES -- for customers with NZ operations who use
  third-party data inputs into their consent or targeting layer.
  Recommend advisory note within 60 days.

---------------------------------------------------------------
ID-PRIVACY IMPACT
---------------------------------------------------------------
Affected subsystems:
  [ ] Consent Banner               -- No direct banner change required; IPP 3A
                                      is a notification obligation, not a
                                      consent requirement per se.
  [ ] Cookie Classification ML     -- Not affected.
  [ ] Consent Storage & Proof      -- Not directly affected; IPP 3A notification
                                      is a separate compliance action from
                                      consent capture.
  [ ] Consent Signals (GPC/TCF/GPP)-- Not affected.
  [X] Geo / Jurisdiction Routing   -- NZ ruleset should be flagged in the
                                      admin UI for customers: if a customer
                                      uses third-party data enrichment for NZ
                                      traffic, they have a new IPP 3A
                                      notification obligation. No platform code
                                      change; configuration documentation update
                                      only.
  [X] Vendor / SDK Governance      -- Where a vendor or SDK provides third-party
                                      audience data for NZ users, customers must
                                      ensure IPP 3A notification has been given.
                                      ID-PRIVACY vendor governance module should
                                      flag NZ-applicable vendors that supply
                                      third-party personal data and prompt
                                      customers to confirm their IPP 3A
                                      notification process.
  [ ] DSR Engine                   -- No new DSR rights or response-window
                                      changes from IPP 3A.
  [ ] Audit / Logging              -- Not affected.
  [ ] Cross-border transfer        -- Not affected.
  [ ] Tenant Config / Admin UI     -- No new tenant config required; NZ is a
                                      small jurisdiction handled via existing
                                      APAC routing. Documentation note only.
  [X] Documentation / DPIA / RoPA  -- RoPA template for NZ operations should
                                      add a column / field for IPP 3A
                                      notification status for indirect collection
                                      flows. Customer-facing DPIA questionnaire
                                      should ask whether third-party data feeds
                                      are used for NZ traffic.

RISK IF NOT ACTIONED
---------------------------------------------------------------
Low risk at platform level; ID-PRIVACY's CMP does not directly breach IPP 3A.
Risk is concentrated at customer level if they use third-party data enrichment for
NZ users without notifying subjects. OPC can issue compliance notices and refer
serious cases for prosecution. Maximum fine under the Privacy Act 2020 is NZD
10,000 (relatively low). Reputational risk to DataSafeguard if a customer is
found non-compliant in a jurisdiction where ID-PRIVACY's vendor governance module
did not flag the obligation.
