WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
On 2026-06-29 the US Supreme Court ruled 6-3 that FTC commissioners can be removed by the President at will, overturning the 91-year-old Humphrey's Executor precedent that guaranteed for-cause removal protection. The European Commission's 2023 adequacy decision underpinning the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension) leans on FTC independence as core evidence that US oversight is "essentially equivalent" to EU protections — noyb counts over 250 references to that independence in the Commission's decision. On 2026-06-30, noyb (Max Schrems) sent the Commission a formal letter demanding an "orderly withdrawal" from the DPF adequacy decision and warned it will file its own annulment action within weeks if the Commission does not act. The adequacy decision remains formally in force today, but its legal foundation is now under acute and credible challenge, in parallel with the already-pending Latombe appeal at the CJEU (Case C-703/25 P).
