WHAT CHANGED (<=4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Chile's Law 21.719 replaces the 1999 data protection law with a GDPR-style regime (consent and other legal bases, data subject rights, a dedicated enforcement agency with fining powers). The transitional article sets entry into force at the 24th month after publication, i.e. 2026-12-01. The 60-day pre-effective window was crossed on 2026-10-02. A government bill filed to postpone entry into force by one year (to 2027-12-01) is reported as still in first reading with no votes, so the date remains legally 2026-12-01 today. We found no cookie-specific rule in the sources reviewed; the Agency is reported to be expected to issue guidance.
