WHAT CHANGED (<=4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TCF v2.3, published by IAB Europe on 19 June 2025, made the disclosedVendors segment of the TC string mandatory: this is a bitfield that records, for each vendor in the Global Vendor List, whether that vendor was actually displayed to the user in the CMP interface (bit=1) versus not disclosed (bit=0). Under TCF v2.2 this segment was optional, meaning downstream vendors receiving a consent string could not reliably distinguish "user saw this vendor and consented" from "CMP generated a consent signal but never showed the vendor to the user," which undermined the transparency purpose of the framework and was a known compliance weakness flagged by DPAs. The mandatory deadline was 28 February 2026: after that date, TC strings generated without a disclosedVendors segment are considered invalid by the IAB TCF governance rules, and Google confirmed in October 2025 that its systems would treat such strings as invalid and fall back to Limited Ads (non-personalised), causing severe CPM impact. This deadline passed 83 days ago; any ID-PRIVACY CMP deployment still generating TCF v2.2 strings is causing immediate, measurable revenue loss for affected customers and is out of specification with the IAB framework.
