───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Chile moves from a bare consent-only regime (Ley 19.628) to a GDPR-aligned, multi-legal-basis framework. For cookies/consent specifically: pre-checked or implied consent boxes are now explicitly prohibited; consent for purposes beyond strict service execution (marketing, behavioral/profiling analytics, third-party sharing) must be separate, explicit, and unbundled from service-necessary processing; a cookie banner is mandatory wherever analytics or advertising cookies are used; and data subjects must have a simple, free, accessible mechanism to withdraw consent at any time. A new independent regulator, the Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales (APDP), stands up alongside the effective date with fines up to 20,000 UTM and is tasked with issuing forthcoming cookie-specific guidelines (not yet published).
