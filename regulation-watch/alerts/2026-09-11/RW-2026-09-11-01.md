WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PIPA's administrative fine ceiling for severe or repeat violations rises from 3% to up to 10% of a company's total (not just relevant) revenue, applicable where a controller commits a repeat serious/grossly-negligent violation within three years, a violation affects 10 million or more individuals, or a controller ignores a PIPC corrective order and a breach results. The amendment also statutorily designates the CEO/representative as the "ultimately responsible person" for privacy governance, with board-reporting obligations on CPO appointment/removal, and reaffirms a 72-hour breach-notification duty triggered on awareness of the *possibility* of a breach. A new mandatory fine-reduction credit rewards demonstrable proactive investment in privacy budget/personnel. Mandatory ISMS-P certification for large-scale controllers follows later, from 2027-07-01.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHO IS AFFECTED
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Applicability test: PIPA applies extraterritorially (Art. 3) to any controller processing personal data of Korea-based data subjects, including via cookies/SDKs on Korea-facing digital properties, regardless of where the controller is established.
- DataSafeguard customers likely affected: any tenant with Korea-facing web/app properties setting cookies, trackers, or SDKs that touch Korean visitors' personal data.
