WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Peru's phased DPO mandate under Law 29733's implementing regulation reaches its second tier: "medium" enterprises must appoint and register a Data Protection Officer by 2026-11-30, following "large" enterprises which were already required to do so from 2025-11-30. This is primarily a compliance-program (governance) requirement rather than a technical change to cookie/consent capture, but the DPO becomes the accountable contact reflected in privacy notices, cookie banners, and RoPA records, and the internal escalation point for consent-related data-subject requests. The underlying regulation (in force since 2025-03-30) also introduced ISO/IEC 27001-aligned security-measure expectations and tighter breach-notification timelines, but those are not new as of this deadline.
