WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Secondary compliance-vendor sources report that the SIC is moving to formalize Colombia's existing prohibition on pre-ticked/implied cookie consent (already established under prior SIC guidance and Habeas Data doctrine) into an enforceable, dated opt-in standard for non-essential cookies, effective 2026-12-13. This is reported alongside continued active SIC cookie-enforcement (a previously reported ~$214M COP fine in 2025) and two separate pending congressional reform bills (214/2025, 274/2025) that would raise Colombia's maximum privacy fines to 5% of annual operating revenue — those bills are a distinct, not-yet-enacted development and are NOT the subject of this alert.
