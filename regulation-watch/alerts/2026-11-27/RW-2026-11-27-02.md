WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The Consent Manager registration regime went live on 2026-11-13: eligible India-incorporated companies (reported minimum net worth INR 2 crore) can now apply to the Board for registration via its portal. Consent Managers will be the interoperable layer through which Data Principals give, manage and withdraw consent. This does not yet impose consent-collection duties on tenants; those start 2027-05-13. A minor date discrepancy (12/13/14 Nov) exists in some secondary sources; most and the PIB release say 13 Nov.
