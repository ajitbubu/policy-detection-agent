WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PIPA's fine ceiling for high-severity violations (intentional/grossly-negligent repeat offenses within 3 years, incidents affecting ≥10M individuals, or non-compliance with a PIPC corrective order) jumps to 10% of total company revenue — a categorical change from the prior fixed/relevant-revenue model. The amendment newly designates the CEO/business owner as the "ultimate responsible person" for data protection and requires certain organizations to report CPO designations to the PIPC. Breach-notification triggers expand to cover forgery, alteration, and damage, and in some cases require notice on a "meaningful possibility" of an incident rather than confirmed harm. ISMS-P certification, previously voluntary, becomes mandatory for designated large-scale controllers from 2027-07-01. This does not add new cookie-consent UI requirements directly, but existing PIPA cookie-consent obligations (opt-in for non-essential cookies, installation/rejection disclosures) now sit behind a materially larger enforcement stick.
