WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Building on PIPL's Jan 1, 2026 certification pathway for mid-scale cross-border personal-information exporters, CAC/SAMR's national standard GB/T 46068-2025 plus a second wave of supplementary standards impose concrete, evidentiary technical requirements effective July 1, 2026. Exporters must now demonstrate — not merely document — encryption in transit, access-control specifications, and audit logging, with cross-border transfer logs retained for at least three years. Previously, a written policy and PIPIA sufficed for the certification route; now regulators expect an "evergreen evidence repository" (network diagrams, access-control policies, incident-response records). This directly affects any tenant moving cookie/tracking-derived consent or PI data out of China via the certification pathway.
