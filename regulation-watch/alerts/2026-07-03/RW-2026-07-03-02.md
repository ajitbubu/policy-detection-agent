WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Korea's National Assembly passed a major PIPA amendment (promulgated 2026-03-10, effective 2026-09-11) that raises the maximum administrative fine from 3% to 10% of total revenue for willful/repeated violations or breaches affecting 10 million+ data subjects, imposes personal supervisory liability on the CEO, tightens board-approval requirements for Chief Privacy Officer appointment/removal (for large controllers), and expands mandatory breach notification to cover "possibility of a breach" (not just confirmed loss/theft/leakage) plus forgery/alteration/damage. On 2026-06-02 the PIPC opened a draft Enforcement Decree amendment for public comment to operationalize thresholds (which controllers count as "large," CPO governance mechanics, ISMS-P certification scope). The old rule capped fines at 3% and required only confirmed-incident notification; the new rule is a step-change in both financial exposure and evidentiary/audit-trail burden. This does not change cookie-consent-capture mechanics directly, but it substantially raises the stakes on the audit-trail and CPO-governance evidence that ID-PRIVACY's consent/audit subsystems must be able to produce for Korean tenants.
