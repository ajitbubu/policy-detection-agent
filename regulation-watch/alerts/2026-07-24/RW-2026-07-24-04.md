WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Korea passed the most consequential PIPA amendment since the 2023 overhaul: administrative fines for serious violations can now reach 10% of total company revenue (previously a much lower ceiling), the CEO/business representative is designated the "ultimate responsible person" for data protection with personal supervisory liability, and breach-notification triggers were broadened to cover forgery/alteration/damage to personal information and "meaningful possibility" of an incident — not just confirmed breaches. PIPA's existing cookie/consent rules (opt-in consent required for non-essential tracking cookies, no notice-and-opt-out shortcut) are not changed by this amendment; the delta here is fines, accountability, and breach-notification scope, not the consent-capture mechanic itself. Qualifying organizations must also obtain board approval for CPO appointments and report CPO changes to the PIPC.
