───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
China has completed the three-pathway framework for cross-border transfer of personal information under PIPL: security assessment (high-volume/sensitive), standard contract (mid/low-volume), and now certification — a third, auditable route via CAC/SAMR-authorized certification bodies, live since 1 January 2026 with bodies officially authorized to accept applications from 30 December 2025. This gives mid-scale exporters (who don't qualify for Standard Contract but fall short of Security Assessment thresholds) a defined compliance pathway that previously did not formally exist. Existing PIPL consent requirements for cross-border transfer of tracking/cookie-derived data (explicit, separate consent) are unchanged by this measure — it only adds a new mechanism for the transfer itself, not a new consent-capture requirement.
