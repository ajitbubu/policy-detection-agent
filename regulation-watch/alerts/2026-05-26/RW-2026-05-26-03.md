WHAT CHANGED (plain English)
---------------------------------------------------------------
The CPPA's revised CCPA/CPRA regulation package, effective January 1, 2026, introduced
a materially new requirement on top of the existing GPC/opt-out signal obligations:
businesses must now provide consumers with a visible, verifiable CONFIRMATION that
their opt-out preference signal (including GPC) has been processed and honoured.
Previously, businesses were required to detect and act on GPC but had no obligation
to confirm that action to the user. The January 2026 regulations make the confirmation
step mandatory: on receipt of a GPC or equivalent opt-out signal, the business must
display a clear indicator (e.g., a persistent UI element, a status message, or a
preference-centre state) showing the signal was received and applied.

The CPPA has already demonstrated it will enforce this actively: in February 2026 it
settled with Disney for USD 2.75M, citing among other violations Disney's failure to
propagate GPC signals consistently across logged-in vs logged-out states. Two further
enforcement actions totalling nearly USD 1.5M (Ford, PlayOn Sports) followed in early
2026. The CPPA's enforcement is now coordinated with Colorado and Connecticut AGs on
GPC compliance, signalling a multi-state sweep. The 2026 regulations also require
that opt-out decisions be propagated to all downstream analytics tools, adtech partners,
and service providers -- not just blocked at the first-party data collection point.

---------------------------------------------------------------
WHO IS AFFECTED
---------------------------------------------------------------
Applicability test:    CCPA/CPRA thresholds: businesses that (a) have >USD 25M annual
                       gross revenue, OR (b) buy/sell/share personal data of 100,000+
                       CA consumers or households, OR (c) derive 50%+ revenue from
                       selling personal data. Businesses that are subject and deploy
                       any tracking or targeted advertising to California residents.
DataSafeguard customers likely affected: All US enterprise customers who are CCPA-
                       covered businesses; estimated majority of US commercial accounts.
                       Any customer not yet confirming GPC receipt to end users is
                       non-compliant today.
Customer notice required:  YES -- immediate. Customers should have remediated by Jan 1;
                       those who have not are currently exposed. Priority advisory.

ID-PRIVACY IMPACT
---------------------------------------------------------------
Affected subsystems:
  [X] Consent Banner               -- After detecting a GPC signal (or equivalent opt-out
                                      preference signal), the banner/preference centre
                                      must render a visible confirmation state: e.g.,
                                      "Your opt-out has been applied" with a persistent
                                      indicator. This must appear on the current session
                                      and on return visits for CA-scoped users.
                                      The Disney enforcement specifically flagged that
                                      logged-in users' GPC signals were not propagated
                                      across the account -- confirmation must be
                                      account-level, not just device-level, for logged-in
                                      contexts.
  [ ] Cookie Classification ML     -- No classifier change required directly.
  [X] Consent Storage & Proof      -- GPC signal receipt and the confirmation event must
                                      both be stored in the consent record: gpc_detected
                                      (bool), gpc_detected_at (timestamp), gpc_confirmed_at
                                      (timestamp), gpc_propagated_to_vendors (bool).
                                      This creates an audit trail demonstrating the
                                      confirmation step was completed.
  [X] Consent Signals (GPC/TCF/GPP) -- Core change: the GPC detection pipeline must emit
                                       a confirmation event back to the UI layer. The
                                       GPP string for California (SID 7 / US National)
                                       must reflect the opt-out state in a way visible
                                       to all downstream vendors in the consent signal
                                       chain. Downstream propagation to adtech/analytics
                                       vendors via the GPP string or direct API call
                                       must be verifiable and logged.
  [X] Geo / Jurisdiction Routing   -- CA jurisdiction ruleset must activate the
                                      GPC-confirmation requirement. This must also
                                      activate for Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA),
                                      and other states that mandate GPC recognition --
                                      the CPPA/Colorado/Connecticut multi-state sweep
                                      makes cross-state consistency essential.
  [X] Vendor / SDK Governance      -- Vendors in the ID-PRIVACY vendor list must have
                                      a CA opt-out propagation status flag. The platform
                                      must be able to demonstrate that a GPC signal
                                      was propagated to each vendor, not just the
                                      first-party layer. New vendor attribute needed:
                                      gpc_propagation_supported (bool) + propagation_method.
  [ ] DSR Engine                   -- Opt-out of sale/sharing via GPC is a DSR-adjacent
                                      right; confirm DSR engine treats a GPC signal as
                                      a standing opt-out-of-sale request and stores it.
                                      (Likely already implemented; verify.)
  [X] Audit / Logging              -- New mandatory audit fields: GPC detection event,
                                      confirmation event, vendor propagation events,
                                      logged-in vs logged-out state at detection time.
  [ ] Cross-border transfer        -- Not directly affected.
  [X] Tenant Config / Admin UI     -- New CA-jurisdiction setting: "GPC confirmation
                                      display enabled" (must default ON for CA). Allow
                                      customers to configure the confirmation message
                                      text and display style.
  [X] Documentation / DPIA / RoPA  -- CCPA-specific RoPA entries must document the
                                      GPC propagation mechanism and confirmation step.

    settlement implies a functional requirement but does not specify exact UX. Legal
    interpretation needed.

  - Vendor propagation depends on vendor API availability; some vendors may not
    support real-time opt-out propagation.

RISK IF NOT ACTIONED
---------------------------------------------------------------
CPPA maximum fine: USD 7,500 per intentional violation; USD 2,500 per violation.
In practice, the Disney settlement of USD 2.75M demonstrates multi-violation aggregation.
The multi-state coordination with Colorado and Connecticut means a single platform gap
can trigger enforcement in three jurisdictions simultaneously. DataSafeguard customers
who cannot demonstrate GPC confirmation and downstream propagation are at material
enforcement risk today. DataSafeguard's own platform, if it enables customers to process
California users without the confirmation step, may be characterised as a facilitating
tool in enforcement proceedings.
