WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Korea's PIPA previously capped administrative fines well below global-turnover levels. The amendment authorizes the PIPC to impose fines of up to 10% of total company revenue for intentional or grossly negligent violations that are repeated within three years, affect 10 million+ individuals, or follow non-compliance with a PIPC corrective order plus a resulting breach. It also formally designates the CEO/business representative as the "ultimate responsible person" for data-protection compliance, requires reporting of CPO designations, and expands breach-notification triggers to cover forgery/alteration/damage events. This does not change PIPA's existing cookie/tracking opt-in consent requirements directly, but it dramatically raises the financial and personal-liability stakes of any existing consent-compliance gap.
