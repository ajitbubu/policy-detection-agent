WHAT CHANGED (plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
South Korea passed the most significant overhaul of PIPA since its 2023 rewrite. The National Assembly voted on 12 February 2026 and the President promulgated on 10 March 2026, with most provisions taking effect 11 September 2026. The prior maximum fine was 3% of revenue tied to the violation; the amended law introduces a second tier of up to 10% of total annual turnover for intentional/gross-negligence repeat violations affecting 10 million+ individuals or arising from non-compliance with a PIPC corrective order. For cookies and online tracking, the amendment explicitly requires that privacy policies disclose the installation, operation, and rejection options for "automatic personal information collection devices" (the statute's term for cookies, pixels, and SDKs), and when cookie-derived data is combinable with other data to identify an individual, explicit opt-in consent is required. The prior rule was that disclosure in a privacy policy alone was considered sufficient in many implementation guides.
