WHAT CHANGED (plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
On 7 April 2026, the Japanese Cabinet approved an APPI amendment bill and submitted it to the National Diet for consideration. This is a bill, not yet enacted law. The most material provision for ID-PRIVACY: the bill would bring cookie IDs and other "contactable personally referable information" (phone numbers, email addresses, cookie identifiers that enable direct contact with a specific individual) under new restrictions (proposed Article 31-2), even when they do not meet the APPI's current definition of "personal information." This closes a long-standing gap where behavioral cookie profiles were largely unregulated if not directly linked to a named individual. If enacted, third-party cookies that enable direct targeting of individuals would require consent or another lawful basis even absent linkage to personal information. The bill also introduces parental consent requirements for children under 16 and a statistical processing exemption for AI. Effective date, if passed, is within two years of promulgation — the earliest practical enforcement date is approximately 2028.
