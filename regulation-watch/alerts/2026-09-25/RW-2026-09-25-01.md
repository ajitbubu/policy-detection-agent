WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Korea's PIPA amendment (effective 2026-09-11) hardens the cookie/tracking consent standard: previously a notice-then-opt-out disclosure was widely treated as sufficient for behavioral/identifiable cookies; now specific, informed, prior (opt-in) consent is required, and non-essential cookies must be technically blocked until the visitor affirmatively opts in. Separately, the amendment raises the maximum administrative fine from 3% to 10% of total company revenue for repeated/willful/grossly-negligent violations or violations affecting 10M+ data subjects, and designates the business owner/representative as the "ultimate responsible person" for compliance. This is a live, in-force rule change, not a future deadline — any tenant currently running a Korea banner in notice-only or opt-out mode is non-compliant today.
