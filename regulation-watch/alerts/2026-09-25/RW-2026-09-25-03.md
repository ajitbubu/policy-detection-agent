WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Nothing new in the underlying rule this week — this is a previously-published phased compliance calendar under Peru's 2024 data protection regulation crossing our 90-day early-warning window. Large companies (>2,300 UIT revenue) already had to appoint a Data Protection Officer by 2025-11-30; medium companies (1,700–2,300 UIT) must do the same by 2026-11-30, with small/micro companies following in 2027/2028. The DPO obligation itself is a controller-side governance requirement (documentation, named point of contact, escalation channel for regulator and data-subject requests) rather than a change to consent-capture mechanics; Peru's existing cookie-consent standard (free, prior, express, informed, unequivocal, per Law 29733) is unchanged.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
