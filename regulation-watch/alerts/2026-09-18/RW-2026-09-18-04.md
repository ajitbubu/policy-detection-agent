WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
As of 2026-09-11, Korea's PIPA now caps administrative fines at 10% of a company's total (not just related) revenue for high-severity cases (intentional/grossly-negligent repeat violations, breaches affecting 10M+ individuals, or non-compliance with a PIPC corrective order that results in a breach). The amendment formally designates the CEO/business owner as the "ultimate person responsible" for data protection, requires board-level approval of CPO appointment/dismissal for qualifying controllers, and phases in mandatory ISMS-P certification (from 2027-07-01) where previously voluntary. Existing cookie/online-tracking consent requirements (explicit opt-in for combinable cookie data, blocking non-essential cookies pending consent) are pre-existing PIPA/Enforcement Decree obligations and are NOT newly created by this amendment — this pass found no new cookie-specific technical mandate tied to the 2026-09-11 date. The material delta for ID-PRIVACY customers is governance/accountability and fine exposure, not a CMP technical flow change.
