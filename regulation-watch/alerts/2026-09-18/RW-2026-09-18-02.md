───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
China's PIPL cross-border transfer regime is now fully operational with all three legal export pathways live: mandatory Security Assessment (CIIOs, or >1M individuals'/>10K sensitive-individuals' PI exported per year), Standard Contract (100K–1M individuals, or <10K sensitive), and — new as of 2026-01-01 — third-party Certification for handlers below the SCC floor who want an alternative to self-executed SCCs. A second wave of mandatory technical standards (accreditation criteria for certification bodies, technical controls, sector rules) came into force 2026-07-01, and CAC published an official Q&A on 2026-07-24 clarifying application of these pathways. Any DataSafeguard customer exporting China-collected identifiers/tracking data (e.g., analytics, ad-tech, consent-log replication to a non-China region) must now map its export volume to the correct pathway — the old "wait and see" posture is no longer defensible since all three routes are codified and enforceable.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHO IS AFFECTED
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Applicability test:  Any personal-information handler (controller) transferring PI collected from individuals in China to a location outside China — includes routine SaaS architectures where consent logs, analytics events, or cookie IDs collected in China are processed/stored on non-China infrastructure.
