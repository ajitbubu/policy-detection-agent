WHAT CHANGED (plain English)
---------------------------------------------------------------
The ICO published the final version of its "Guidance on the use of
storage and access technologies" on 29 April 2026, superseding all
prior ICO cookie guidance and incorporating changes introduced by the
Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA). The prior guidance required
consent for virtually all non-strictly-necessary cookies; the DUAA
and this finalized guidance add five new purpose-specific statutory
exemptions from the consent requirement under PECR Regulation 6,
effective 5 February 2026. The guidance also introduces a new
sub-chapter clarifying that a single technology used for multiple
purposes cannot rely on an exemption for any purpose that does not
independently qualify -- granular consent is required for the
non-exempt use. Additionally, the ICO now requires a "simple means
of objecting" alongside the statistical (analytics) and appearance
exemptions, and confirms the reject-all button must be equally
prominent to accept-all on all consent banners. Separately, the DUAA
has raised the maximum PECR fine from GBP 500,000 to GBP 17.5M or
4% of global annual turnover, placing PECR enforcement at UK GDPR
parity.

ID-PRIVACY IMPACT
---------------------------------------------------------------
Affected subsystems:

  [X] Consent Banner               -- UK jurisdiction rule: reject-all
      button must have equal visual prominence to accept-all on layer 1.
      Verify current UK banner variant passes equal-prominence check.
      Also: add "simple means of objecting" flow for analytics-exempt
      and appearance-exempt cookie deployments (new ICO sub-chapter
      requirement -- users must be able to object without full consent
      revocation).

  [X] Cookie Classification ML     -- Five new DUAA exemption categories
      must be added as distinct classification labels in the UK ruleset:
      (1) communication transmission, (2) user-requested service,
      (3) statistical/analytics (narrow), (4) appearance/preference
      adaptation, (5) emergency assistance identification. Classifier
      must detect dual-use cookies and flag for consent gating on the
      non-exempt purpose. Prior "strictly necessary" taxonomy is
      insufficient.

  [X] Consent Storage & Proof      -- For cookies relying on analytics or
      appearance exemption, the ICO requires a "simple means of
      objecting" to be offered and objections to be honored and logged.
      Proof-of-consent store must be able to record exemption-reliance
      (rather than consent) as the legal basis, along with objection
      records.

  [X] Consent Signals (GPC/TCF/GPP)-- No direct GPC change, but the
      "simple means of objecting" mechanism for exempt cookies must
      integrate with existing signal-processing pipeline so that a user
      objection stops the exempt processing immediately.

  [X] Geo / Jurisdiction Routing   -- UK ruleset must be updated to
      reflect the new five-tier exemption taxonomy. Ensure
      jurisdiction == UK routes to the new ruleset, not the legacy
      "strictly necessary + consent" binary.

  [ ] Vendor / SDK Governance      -- No direct new vendor obligation,
      but dual-use cookie detection affects third-party SDK disclosure
      requirements (downstream impact on audit).

  [ ] DSR Engine                   -- No change.

  [X] Audit / Logging              -- The ICO expects evidence that
      organizations relying on exemptions have documented the purpose
      mapping. Audit log should capture exemption basis per cookie
      firing event in UK sessions.

  [ ] Cross-border transfer        -- No change.

  [X] Tenant Config / Admin UI     -- Add UK-specific toggle: "rely on
      DUAA statistical exemption" (with objection mechanism enabled) and
      "rely on DUAA appearance exemption" (with objection mechanism
      enabled). Each toggle must gate the corresponding ICO compliance
      sub-flows.

  [X] Documentation / DPIA / RoPA  -- Cookie inventory and RoPA entries
      for UK customers must distinguish consent basis vs. exemption
      basis per DUAA category. DPIA template for UK analytics deployments
      needs updating.
