WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TCF Policy v5.0.b introduces three substantive changes CMPs must implement: (1) new rules for how consent signals must be handled when an authenticated user moves between devices (multi-device consent persistence, aligned with CNIL guidance), (2) a rename and clarified scope for "Special Feature 2" — from "Actively scan device characteristics for identification" to "Identify devices based on information actively requested" — to explicitly cover Client Hints-based fingerprinting, and (3) new CMP UI requirements mandating that the standard feature-explanation text be displayed alongside each feature's standard name and full user-friendly text. This is a policy-and-technical-spec amendment to a framework ID-PRIVACY already claims conformance with, not a brand-new law; the "old rule" was TCF v2.3 (mandatory `disclosedVendors` segment, effective 2026-02-28) with Special Feature 2 defined narrowly around active device scanning and no formal multi-device consent-continuity rule.

RISK IF NOT ACTIONED

Non-compliant CMPs risk removal from the IAB Europe CMP List / Global Vendor List, which invalidates TCF strings for every publisher relying on that CMP registration — a functional outage of programmatic ad monetization for affected tenants, not merely a compliance paperwork issue. No known regulator (CNIL, EDPB, etc.) has yet issued a binding order tied specifically to v5.0.b, so legal/DPA exposure is currently indirect — the direct risk is commercial/functional via the IAB Europe conformance program.
