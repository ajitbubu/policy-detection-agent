───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT CHANGED (plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
On 29 May 2026, IAB Europe and IAB Tech Lab opened a 30-day public comment
period on a set of amendments that advance the TCF Policy from v5.0.a to
v5.0.b. The prior policy (v5.0.a, implemented alongside TCF v2.3 with the
February 2026 deadline) left three gaps: (1) how CMPs must resolve conflicting
consent signals when an authenticated user has given different consent choices
on different devices; (2) what explanatory text CMPs must display alongside
each feature listed in the consent UI (new Appendix B(B)(f) requirement); and
(3) the name and vendor guidance for Special Feature 2 ("Actively scan device
characteristics for identification" is renamed to "Identify devices based on
information actively requested," with updated vendor guidance clarifying that
active requesting of Client Hints for fingerprinting purposes falls within its
scope). None of these are cosmetic — change (1) requires new signal-resolution
logic in the CMP runtime, change (2) requires new text fields in the UI layer,
and change (3) requires updated GVL integration when the revised GVL ships
(estimated mid-July 2026). CMPs that are not compliant by mid-October 2026
risk losing their registered CMP status under the TCF.
