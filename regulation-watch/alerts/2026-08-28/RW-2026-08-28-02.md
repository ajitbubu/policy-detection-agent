WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
China has completed its cross-border personal information (PI) transfer regulatory framework under PIPL: as of 2026-01-01, the third and final legal pathway — third-party certification — is fully operational alongside the pre-existing Security Assessment and Standard Contract routes. This certification route is specifically designed for mid-scale exporters (below the mandatory CAC Security Assessment thresholds, e.g. sensitive PI of 10,000+ individuals or 1M+ individuals generally, but too large or otherwise ineligible for the Standard Contract route) — a segment that previously had no clean compliance path. Applicants must show notice-and-consent compliance, a completed PI protection impact assessment, and certification by a qualified third-party body; CIIOs and exporters of "important data" are excluded from this route. Separately, amended PRC Cybersecurity Law provisions effective in 2026 increased administrative penalties for non-compliant cross-border transfers.
