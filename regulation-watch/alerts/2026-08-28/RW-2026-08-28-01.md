WHAT CHANGED (≤4 sentences, plain English)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Korea's National Assembly passed an amendment to PIPA on 2026-02-12 that raises the administrative fine ceiling to up to 10% of a controller's total (global) revenue for the most severe violations, taking effect 2026-09-11. The 10% ceiling applies where a controller (a) intentionally or with gross negligence repeats a violation within 3 years, (b) has a single incident affecting 10M+ data subjects via intentional/grossly negligent conduct, or (c) ignores a PIPC corrective order and a breach results. The amendment also makes the CEO the statutorily designated responsible person for data protection compliance, creating personal accountability at the top of the organization. This does not itself introduce a new cookie/consent capture rule, but it dramatically raises the financial and personal-liability stakes for any consent-related violation (e.g., invalid consent capture, inadequate audit trail, unhonored withdrawal) that a customer commits in Korea after 2026-09-11.
